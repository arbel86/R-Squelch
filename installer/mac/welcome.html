R-Squelch by RBell

A free psytrance squelch synth, made for the community. Create, share, go wild.

This installs:

  • VST3 to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • R-Squelch.app (standalone) to /Applications

After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.13+).
